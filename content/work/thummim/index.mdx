<Section title="Overview">
  Thummim runs healthcare course platforms and a Flutter mobile app for learners across Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana, with regional sites at thummimng.com, thummim.co.ke and thummimgh.com. From January 2022 to September 2025 I was Head of IT, responsible for IT operations and the frontend for the regional offices, on platforms serving 15K+ users.
</Section>

<Section title="Custom WordPress plugins">
  I built custom WordPress plugins in PHP, using the REST API, that cut content management time by 60%.
</Section>

<Section title="Infrastructure and security">
  The sites run behind Cloudflare CDN, with JWT-based authentication. I improved infrastructure, scaling and support processes: support response time went from 8hrs to under 2hrs, and services ran at 99% uptime.
</Section>

<Section title="Leadership">
  I mentored junior developers and set the team's coding standards.
