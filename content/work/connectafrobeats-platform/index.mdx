<Section title="Overview">
  ConnectAfrobeats is a social network for Afrobeats fans: chat, a feed and content creation. From August 2024 to September 2025 I was its frontend engineer and design lead, leading development of the platform in Next.js and TypeScript and working with the wider team to deliver it.
</Section>

<Section title="State and caching architecture">
  I built the app's data layer on Zustand and TanStack Query. That optimisation produced a 30% reduction in initial load time, and the Core Web Vitals score went from 62 to 85 on mobile.
</Section>

<Section title="Chat, feed and content creation">
  The platform's core is social: real-time chat, a feed and tools for fans to create and share content.
</Section>

<Section title="Auth and payments">
  Sign-in uses Google Sign-in and JWT, and payments run through Paystack.
</Section>

<Section title="Accessible redesign">
  As design lead I redesigned the UI with Tailwind and Shadcn UI to meet WCAG 2.1 AA, for a platform with 1K+ active users.

  I also designed the brand's social, newsletter and event creative; see [ConnectAfrobeats Creative](/work/connectafrobeats-creative).
</Section>
