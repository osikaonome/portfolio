<Section title="Overview">
  As design lead at ConnectAfrobeats I produced the brand's creative across channels: social media graphics, newsletter design and event creative, in Photoshop and Illustrator.
</Section>

<Section title="Event creative: Connect Afrofest '24">
  For the brand's festival, Connect Afrofest '24 (14 December 2024, The Hangar, Lagos), I designed the event campaign: performer announcement posters and panel session line-ups, carrying sponsor and partner marks without losing the festival's own identity.
</Section>

<Section title="Recurring social series">
  Between events, the feed ran on recurring formats with a shared visual system of the brand's gold, green and warm orange, bold display type and the ConnectAfrobeats mark:

  - **Women in Afrobeats:** profiles of women working in the industry
  - **New Music Friday:** weekly release posts
  - **Tuesday Trivia:** interactive quiz cards
  - **Mentorship Programme:** calls for emerging talent, such as aspiring artist managers
</Section>

<Section title="One brand, product to poster">
  I also led the platform's engineering and its accessible UI redesign, so the same brand ran from the app to the feed to the event flyer; see [the platform case study](/work/connectafrobeats-platform).
</Section>
