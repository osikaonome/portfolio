Canvas 2D, no dependencies. Colours follow the time of day in Dublin, read with `Intl.DateTimeFormat`, so there's no API call.

On small screens the pixel ratio is capped at 1 and frames at 30fps. It pauses offscreen, and draws a single static frame for reduced motion, data saver or low-power devices.
